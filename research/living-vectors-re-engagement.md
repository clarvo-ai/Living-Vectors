# Living Vectors: Re-engagement

**Progress / topic:** Re-engagement ideas for the platform — collecting strategies to re-engage inactive users and encourage recurring usage.

---

## Framework (discussion prompts)

### 1. What kind of messages can we send to users who have used our app?

Think about different user segments (job seekers, talent seekers, new users, etc.) and what would be relevant or valuable for each of them.

### 2. What messages does the Jack and Jill application send?

Investigate and compile examples of re-engagement strategies used by Jack and Jill. Focus on:

- Timing (daily/weekly)
- Content (matches, reminders, updates)
- Personalization level

### 3. What would bring you back to the platform?

Reflect on what personally would make you return — it could be new content, social proof, progress, or a sense of urgency.

### 4. What has brought you back to a platform in another app?

Think of apps you stopped using and then returned to. What triggered your return? Was it a message, a new feature, or something else?

### 5. What has worked in other apps?

- **Duolingo** — Gamified streaks and reminder nudges.
- **LinkedIn** — Notifications about profile views and job matches.
- **Airbnb** — Personalized travel suggestions and host updates.
- **Spotify** — Tailored playlists and discovery emails.
- **Notion** — Weekly digest of shared content and new templates.

**Takeaway:** Personalized and meaningful recommendations can be a powerful re-engagement tool.

## Answers & ideas (working notes)

### 1. Job seekers: notifications

**1.1 New matching opportunities**

- “We’ve found 3 new roles that match your profile this week.”

**1.2 Progress and momentum**

- “You’ve applied to 5 jobs this week.”

### 2. Talent seekers / hiring side: notifications

**2.1 Candidate highlights**

- “3 candidates in your pool are strong matches for role X.”

### 3. Dormant users

**3.1 Changes since last visit**

- “Since you last visited, X new roles were posted that match your profile.”

### 4. Timing (inspired by “Jack and Jill”)

**4.1 Event-based**

- New match  
- New message  
- Profile view / interest  

**4.2 Cadence-based**

- Weekly digest summarising new roles and user progress  

### 5. Direct contact feature (Rolodex)

**5.1 Rolodex for employees**

Users can save contact information (email, LinkedIn, etc.) for people in interesting roles — to maximize the utility of Clarvo software and encourage active usage.

**5.1.1 Re-engagement prompt**

- “We found 3 new people related to your target role — add them to your Rolodex and reach out?”

### 6. What has brought me back to apps (feature ideas)

**6.1 Lessons / micro-courses**

- How to negotiate salary, how to advance careers (e.g. strategic job hop).

**6.2 Streaks**

- “You’ve applied to jobs 5 days in a row.” / “You’ve completed a daily quiz for a week in a row.”

**6.3 Job application summary**

- Weekly recap of applied jobs, saved jobs, and profile changes → tracking of responses, rejections, ghosting.

**6.4 Games about professional life**

- Small interactive scenarios / quizzes / lessons about work situations (field-related specifics, e.g. technical questions).

**6.5 Forward job features**

- Easy way to share or forward jobs to friends or mentors.
